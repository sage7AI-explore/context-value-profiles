This work studies how much each kind of context contributes to an LLM agent's task success and how to send less of it.
Sending fewer prompt tokens can lower inference cost and energy use. The main risk is that pruning can remove information
an agent needs, such as constraints or safety instructions. In our implementation, instructions and the goal are
mandatory blocks that no policy may drop, and we recommend that deployments mark any safety-relevant context as
mandatory in the same way. All experiments ran on local open-weight models. The data are public benchmarks used under
their licenses (HotpotQA, CC BY-SA 4.0; BFCL, Apache-2.0) and synthetic conversation histories that contain no personal
data. No human subjects were involved.
